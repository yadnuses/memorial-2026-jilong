Sources & Method

This work uses public reporting, public registration systems, and openly licensed imagery. Historical photographs are used only to show the port's physical setting and daily life, and are not presented as images from the day of the disaster. The maps come from open data and disclose no sensitive information; the simulated route is for demonstration only.

This page accompanies a work of data journalism. Copyright in reporting screenshots and historical photographs remains with the original authors or outlets.
